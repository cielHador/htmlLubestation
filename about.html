Our Story

Since 1995, Lubrication Station has been keeping Saskatoon on the road. What started out as "10 Minute Oil Change" has grown into three locations across the city and Saskatoon's go-to name for fast, reliable quick-lube service.

You'll find us behind the big yellow doors at:

  • 614 Circle Drive East
  • 1-3401 8th Street East
  • 420-22nd Street West

We're independently owned and operated, and we serve drivers throughout Saskatoon, Warman, Martensville, Clavet, and the surrounding area — no appointment needed, Monday through Saturday, 8am to 6pm.

New Ownership, Decades of Experience

In March 2020, Don Lepp — with more than 30 years in the automotive business — purchased Lubrication Station. He brought with him a team that has spent years maintaining and servicing everything from daily commuters to fleet vehicles, import and domestic alike.

What We Do

  • Oil changes
  • Transfer case services
  • Front and rear differential services
  • Transmission services
  • Cooling system services
  • Fuel filter replacements

We understand what makes import and domestic vehicles tick, and we know that using the right product matters just as much as the service itself. That's why we work with trusted names like Shell, Quaker State, Pennzoil, AMSOIL, Royal Purple, ACDelco, and Wynn's — including Wynn's flushes that come backed by a warranty to protect your investment.

We're also proud to service fleet vehicles, helping local businesses stay on the road without unexpected breakdowns.

Why Drivers Choose Us

At Lubrication Station, you get a semi-synthetic blend oil change for the price of a conventional one — and we consistently offer the lowest price on a basic oil change in Saskatoon.

But it's not just about the oil. It's about people. Our team's knowledge and genuine care for your vehicle are what keep customers coming back.

Our mission is simple: unparalleled service, every visit. We pledge our best effort to make your experience both beneficial and enjoyable — try us once, and we're confident you'll be back.
