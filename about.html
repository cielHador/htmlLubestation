How we got here

From "10 Minute Oil Change" to Saskatoon's premier quick lube

Lubrication Station started under the name "10 Minute Oil Change" and has grown into three independently owned and operated locations across the city.

In March 2020, Don Lepp with more than 30 years in the automotive business took over Lubrication Station. He brought a staff with years of experience maintaining and servicing a wide range of vehicles, and an understanding of what import and domestic engines need to run right.

We're proud to service fleet vehicles too, keeping local businesses on the road without unexpected breakdowns.
